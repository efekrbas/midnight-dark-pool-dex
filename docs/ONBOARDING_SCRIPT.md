### Step 1: Set Up Lace Wallet
1. If you don't have it yet, install the [Lace Wallet Browser Extension](https://www.lace.io/).
2. Create a new wallet and save your seed phrase.
3. In the wallet settings, switch your network to **Midnight Preprod**.
4. Go to the [Midnight Faucet](https://faucet.preprod.midnight.network/), paste your wallet address, and request test tokens (tNIGHT). They will arrive in ~2 minutes.

### Step 2: Make a Private Trade on the DEX
1. Go to our Live Demo: [YOUR PREPROD URL HERE]
2. Click **Connect Wallet** and approve the connection in Lace.
3. Choose a trading pair and enter a limit price and order size.
4. Click **Submit Hidden Order**. (You will notice a glowing animation—this is your browser generating the Zero-Knowledge proof so your order details are never sent to the network in plain text!)
5. Approve the transaction in your wallet.

### Step 3: Confirm Your Wallet Address
Once your transaction is submitted, please reply to this message and paste your **Lace Preprod Wallet Address** so I can verify you on-chain and add you to our official `LAUNCH_USERS.md` list!

Let me know if you run into any issues. Happy trading! 🌑
