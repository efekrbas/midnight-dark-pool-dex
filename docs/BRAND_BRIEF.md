# Brand Brief: Midnight Dark Pool DEX

## One-Line Tagline
"Institutional-grade liquidity, complete privacy."

## 3 Key Messages
1. **Uncompromised Privacy:** Your limit prices and order sizes are completely hidden from the public order book using Midnight's Zero-Knowledge circuits.
2. **MEV Resistance:** By keeping order details encrypted until the exact moment of execution, we eliminate front-running and predatory trading bots.
3. **Institutional Scale on Web3:** Trade massive volume with confidence. The blurred depth chart provides market sentiment without revealing your hand.

## Suggested Color Palette
- **Primary Background:** `#0B0E14` (Deep Midnight Blue/Black)
- **Primary Accent:** `#7000FF` (Neon Purple - Represents the Midnight Network)
- **Secondary Accent:** `#00E5FF` (Cyan - Used for active UI elements and glowing ZK-proof states)
- **Text:** `#E2E8F0` (Slate Gray/White)
